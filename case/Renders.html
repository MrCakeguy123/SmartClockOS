SmartClockOS wall case v2 — renders

Full walkthrough of how it goes together lives in AssemblyDiagram.html.

Front — what you see on the wall

Bezel window with the stepped reveal, sensor chin at the bottom right with its grille. USB and every opening face away from the viewer.

Back — cover on

Keyholes (slot up = slides down to lock), fan intake grille, passive vent column, and the two snap hooks visible in their bottom slots.

Shell interior

Flat corner pads instead of v1's pins — nothing left to snap off. Reinforced pads frame the two snap slots in the bottom wall; the retention lips run under the top rim. USB mark engraved on the floor.

Back cover, print orientation

Solid clamp towers (v1's were hollow tubes), fan bay with filleted pins, and the two beefy snap arms — 13 × 2.4 mm now, more than double the old cross-section.

Cutaway

Board resting on the corner pads, glass against the foam lip, towers clamping from behind, tongue-under-lip joint at the top edge (left), USB throat through the left wall.

Straight into the USB funnel

The port mock sits centered with clearance on every side — position taken from the manufacturer drawing (54 mm from the board's top edge), not eyeballed. ±8 mm of slop before any cable would touch plastic.

Exploded assembly

Red: board drops in. Orange: cover goes in tilted, top edge first, then the bottom swings down and clicks twice. Green: sensor into the chin.
